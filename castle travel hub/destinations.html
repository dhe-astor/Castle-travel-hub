Explore Kerala

From misty hills and serene backwaters to sun-kissed beaches and wild adventures.

Hill Station

Munnar

Situated at 1,600m above sea level, Munnar features sprawling green tea estates, clean mountain streams, and the majestic Anamudi peak.

Backwaters

Alleppey (Alappuzha)

Renowned for houseboat cruises along the tranquil backwaters, lush paddy fields, and iconic coconut tree fringed shorelines.

Forest & Wildlife

Wayanad

A bio-diverse hill district offering spiced plantations, waterfalls, ancient Edakkal caves, wild elephant spotting, and peak trekking.

Beach

Kovalam

A coastal town famous for three adjacent crescent beaches, an iconic red-and-white lighthouse, and delicious coastal seafood.

Forest & Wildlife

Thekkady (Periyar)

Home to the famous Periyar Tiger Reserve, cardamom plantations, boat safaris, and spectacular wild elephant watching trails.

Beach

Varkala

Featuring a unique cliffside coastline next to the Arabian Sea, golden sands, natural springs, and a relaxed, bohemian vibe.

Waterfalls

Athirappilly Waterfalls

Known as the "Niagara of India", these majestic 80-foot high waterfalls are nestled within lush green Sholayar forest ranges.

Backwaters

Kumarakom

A cluster of little islands on the Vembanad Lake, featuring rich bird sanctuaries, luxury lake resorts, and peaceful canals.
